# Edit a draft

Run before any draft goes to a person. Ten minutes.

1. **Claims.** Every number, name, quote, and comparison: is it in `notes.md` with a dated source? If
   not, cut it or mark the gap.
2. **Reader.** Read it as someone who has never heard of the company: does it say something true and
   useful, or does it say the company exists?
3. **Voice.** Read it against `knowledge/voice.md`. Cut every sentence that could appear on any other
   company's site.
4. **Promises.** Any guarantee, timeline, price, or outcome? Remove, or mark for a person to decide.
5. **Privacy.** Any private person's details, or a quote without permission on the task? Remove.
6. **Cuts.** Can a paragraph go without losing the point? Cut it. Then cut the opening line and see if
   the piece starts better.
7. **Repeats.** Does it say what the last two pieces said? If so, say it differently or park it.

Record in the task note only what you changed that a person would want to know (claims cut, gaps
marked).
