# Voice

How the company sounds in public. Written by a person; the bot follows it. Keep it short and concrete.

## In a sentence
Plain, specific, and a little dry. We talk like a shop owner explaining it to another shop owner.

## We write
- "Deliveries slip when the schedule lives in three places."
## We do not write
- "Unlock the power of seamless scheduling."

## Rules of thumb
- Short sentences. Numbers only with a source. No exclamation marks in headlines.
- Say what the reader can do, not how excited we are.
- No jargon a shop owner would not use.
