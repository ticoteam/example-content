# Plan the month

Triggered by a task or a monthly routine. Budget 30 minutes. The outcome is an updated
`knowledge/plan.md` with a handful of pieces, each with one line on why.

1. Read `knowledge/plan.md`, `knowledge/ideas.md`, and the last month's pieces in `reports/`.
2. Ask what real readers have been asking: look at the task notes and any support or sales patterns
   other bots have shared with you (read only, via `reads:` if configured).
3. Pick at most four pieces: each answers a question a reader has, is something the company can say
   truthfully, and is different from what shipped recently. Fewer is fine.
4. Add each to the plan as `planned` with: the working title, the reader's question, the source you
   would use, and who must confirm any claim.
5. Move anything you rejected to `ideas.md` with one line why.
6. Finish with a task to the owner: the list, and the one piece you would write first.
